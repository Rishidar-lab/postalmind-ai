## FULL LINKEDIN POST — ENGLISH

Everyone knows the Postman.

Far fewer people understand the workforce structure behind rural postal service.

Gramin Dak Sevaks — GDS — serve through Branch Post Offices across rural India, alongside the wider
network of departmental post offices.

Their engagement runs on its own framework: the GDS (Conduct and Engagement) Rules, 2020, separate
from the rules covering departmental staff. They are paid a Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA)
— a distinct allowance structure, not a regular departmental pay scale.

Depending on the office, duties can include branch postal operations, and business procurement across
small savings, RPLI/PLI insurance and IPPB accounts.

Most people outside the system have never been told this distinction exists — not because it's
hidden, but because it's rarely explained.

PostalMind AI is starting Ground Reality, an evidence-led series, to explain what the record actually
shows about this role — and where it doesn't yet show enough.

This is not a campaign against individual postal employees.

It is an attempt to document a system — with sources, context, counter-evidence and corrections when
we get something wrong.
