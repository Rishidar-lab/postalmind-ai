# 02 — Responsibility Without Parity

## TITLE
RESPONSIBILITY WITHOUT PARITY

## HOOK
The job keeps growing. The employment category it sits in hasn't kept pace with what it's now asked to
carry.

## FULL LINKEDIN POST

RESPONSIBILITY WITHOUT PARITY

Two columns, from the same documented record.

**STATUS**
— Engaged under a separate framework: the GDS (Conduct and Engagement) Rules, 2020 — not the
departmental service rules.
— Paid via Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA), a distinct allowance structure — not a regular
pay scale.
— Working hours assessed by workload slab, not a uniform working day.

**RESPONSIBILITY**
— Handles mail delivery and branch operations.
— Handles savings-bank and financial services — cash, customer accounts, reconciliation.
— Handles IPPB banking.
— Carries small-savings and insurance (RPLI) business-procurement targets.
— Often the only point of postal, banking and insurance contact for a village.

Read those two columns side by side. The right column has grown — financial services, IPPB, business
targets are all comparatively recent additions to what a branch handles. The left column — the
engagement category, the allowance structure, the recognition — is the one from 2016–2020.

This is not a claim that GDS work is identical to a departmental Postman or Postwoman's — the two run
on different rules, and PostalMind is not asserting equivalence it cannot source. It is a narrower,
sourced observation: responsibility on the ground has expanded across postal, financial and commercial
service lines, largely within a framework designed for a narrower role.

So the question worth asking in public, not just in a union hall:

**If responsibility expands across postal, financial and commercial services, how should recognition,
protection and service conditions evolve alongside it?**

This is not a campaign against individual postal employees.

It is a campaign to document the system.

Ground reality. Verified.

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by India Post or the Department of Posts.

## SHORT VERSION
Two columns, same record: STATUS (separate engagement rules, separate TRCA allowance, slab-based
hours) vs RESPONSIBILITY (mail, savings-bank, IPPB, RPLI/business targets — often the only postal,
banking and insurance contact a village has). The right column has grown; the left hasn't moved much
since 2016–2020. Not a claim of job-equivalence with departmental staff — a sourced observation, and a
question: as responsibility expands, how should recognition, protection and service conditions evolve?

## RISK / QUALIFICATION NOTES
- Do not let this post (or any edit of it) imply GDS and departmental Postman/Postwoman roles are
  legally or functionally identical. No source here makes that comparison; PostalMind does not assert
  it.
- "The right column has grown... the left hasn't moved much since 2016–2020" is a *framing* observation
  about which documents are most recent, not a quantified claim about pay or hours changing. Do not
  edit it into a numeric claim (e.g. "pay hasn't risen in N years") without a specific, dated source.
- The closing question is phrased as a question, not a demand — keep it that way; a demand needs a
  specific ask, which belongs in the reform post (`07-what-should-change.md`), not here.
