## ABOUT

I build PostalMind AI — an independent, evidence-led project on the working reality of Gramin Dak
Sevaks (GDS), and a source-grounded AI assistant that will say "I don't know" rather than guess a rule
number.

I bring two things to this that aren't usually found together: I am a serving Gramin Dak Sevak, so the
operational reality — branch work, savings-bank and IPPB duties, business targets, the actual working
conditions the rules describe — is something I do, not something I read about. And I build software:
retrieval-grounded AI systems, evidence-classification pipelines, and privacy-first architecture where
sensitive data never leaves local processing.

PostalMind AI reflects both. It:
— answers GDS questions only from cited, dated sources, and refuses to fabricate a rule or rate it
cannot cite
— helps a worker analyse workplace evidence (WhatsApp exports, documents) with local-first processing,
PII redaction and a reversible redaction map — nothing raw is sent to an AI provider
— gates public export behind a twelve-point safety check, so nothing goes public half-verified
— runs the Ground Reality series, which documents GDS working conditions with the same sourcing
discipline: every claim dated, classified by source type, and paired with what the evidence does
*not* show

I'm not building this to run an outrage account. I'm building it because the gap between what GDS
actually do and what the public understands about that role is real, closable, and better closed with
evidence than with volume.

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by India Post or the Department of Posts.

Open to conversations with technologists, policy people, journalists and union representatives who
care about getting this right rather than getting it loud.
