Ground reality. Verified.

I am starting a series called **Ground Reality** on PostalMind AI — an independent, evidence-led
project on the working reality of Gramin Dak Sevaks (GDS), the rural postal workforce that keeps a
large part of India Post's last-mile service running.

A few things this series will and will not do.

It will:
— explain who GDS are, and how their engagement differs from departmental postal staff
— document duties, working-hour norms and business-target structures as the record actually shows them
— treat workplace-pressure allegations seriously, and separate what is established from what is alleged
— label every claim by source class (official document, committee report, news report, union
allegation, employee testimony, or unverified) and give the date
— say "the evidence does not show this" as plainly as it says "the evidence shows this"
— publish corrections openly if something here turns out to be wrong

It will not:
— claim "all officers abuse GDS," or any other sweeping accusation, without a specific finding behind it
— assert that any death or injury was "caused by" workplace pressure unless an authoritative finding
  says so — allegations are reported as allegations
— invent circular numbers, rule numbers, statistics, death counts or quotations
— publish raw screenshots, phone numbers or names of people who did not consent to being named

I am building this as a serving Gramin Dak Sevak, and as a technologist. Both matter here: the lived
knowledge of the role, and the discipline to check a claim before it goes out under my name.

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by India Post or the Department of Posts.

This is not a campaign against individual postal employees. It is a campaign to document the system.

Ground reality. Verified.
